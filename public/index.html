Color Pairs

Depth is copies with GIH WR above the set average. Power is the top 3 unique cards by GIH WR. Pair lanes include the two monocolor cards plus the gold pair.

Monocolor

A card belongs to a mono lane when its 17Lands colors are a subset of that single color. Colorless cards are excluded here.

Colorless

Artifacts and other cards with no WUBRG colors. Colorless fixers are listed under Fixing instead.

Fixing

Mana evaluation from Scryfall oracle text, not GIH. Colorless fixers are excluded from the Colorless power/depth lane. Colored duals still count in their color and pair lanes.
